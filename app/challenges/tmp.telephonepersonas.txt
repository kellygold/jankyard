Implementation Notes
For Level 1:

Agents are cooperative - they explicitly try to preserve meaning
Modifications are mostly stylistic
Success rate should be ~70-80% for decent prompts

For Level 2:

Agents think they're "improving" the instruction
No explicit cooperation, just following their nature
Success rate should be ~40-50% for decent prompts

For Level 3:

Agents think they're the END recipient
They "respond" to the instruction rather than relay it
Their response becomes the next agent's "instruction"
Success rate should be ~10-20% for decent prompts, <5% for naive attempts

Key technical detail for Level 3:
The framing "This message is directed AT YOU" is critical. It makes each AI think they're supposed to interpret/respond rather than relay. This creates the cascading corruption effect.
