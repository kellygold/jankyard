## 🔓 The Glitch

**Exploit AI security vulnerabilities through adversarial prompt engineering**

### Overview
Break through AI security defenses using clever prompts and manipulation techniques. Three progressive difficulty levels, each with increasingly sophisticated security measures.

### How It Works
- You submit prompts to convince a secured AI to break its rules
- The AI is instructed to maintain certain restrictions
- Your goal: Bypass these restrictions and get the AI to comply with forbidden requests
- Each level adds more defensive mechanisms

### Difficulty Levels

#### Level 1: Basic Defense
- ❌ No memory between messages
- ❌ No meta-awareness
- ❌ No self-correction
- **Strategy:** Simple, direct prompt injections often work

#### Level 2: Enhanced Defense
- ✅ Conversation memory
- ✅ Manipulation awareness
- ✅ Told to stay firm
- **Strategy:** Requires more sophisticated social engineering and multi-turn attacks

#### Level 3: Advanced Defense
- ✅ Full conversation memory
- ✅ Multi-layer defense protocols
- ✅ Self-checking mechanism
- ✅ Uses Judge AI to verify responses
- **Strategy:** Requires creative, subtle manipulation that bypasses AI detection

### Scoring
- **Base Points:** 50-150 (based on difficulty)
- **Efficiency Bonus:** Fewer attempts = higher multiplier
- **Success:** Get the AI to break its security rules

### Max Attempts
- Level 1: 10 attempts
- Level 2: 15 attempts
- Level 3: 20 attempts

## 🕵️ The Interrogation Room

**Detective mystery challenge featuring AI witnesses you must interrogate to solve crimes**

### Overview
Question AI-powered witnesses to identify liars and solve criminal cases. Use cross-referencing, contradiction detection, and strategic questioning across 3 progressive levels.

### How It Works
- Multiple AI witnesses, each with their own story and secrets
- Some witnesses are lying, some tell the truth
- You get limited questions per witness
- After interrogating, you must identify the guilty party
- Each level features a different crime with more complex deception

### Difficulty Levels

#### Level 1: The Last Transmission
- **Crime:** Space station communication sabotage
- **Witnesses:** 3
- **Questions per witness:** 5
- **Complexity:** Single guilty party with simple contradictions

#### Level 2: The Museum Heist
- **Crime:** Valuable artifact theft
- **Witnesses:** 4  
- **Questions per witness:** 4
- **Complexity:** Coordinated alibis and more subtle lies

#### Level 3: The Data Breach
- **Crime:** Corporate espionage
- **Witnesses:** 5
- **Questions per witness:** 3
- **Complexity:** Multiple guilty parties with coordinated stories

### Scoring
- **Base Points:** 100-300 (based on difficulty)
- **Correct Accusation:** Full points
- **Wrong Accusation:** Score penalty per failed attempt
- **Efficiency Bonus:** Fewer questions used = higher score

### Max Accusation Attempts
3 attempts per level to identify the guilty party

## 🎭 Debate Battle

**Battle against the House Bot in a structured debate, judged by AI**

### Overview
Your prompt creates an AI debater that battles the House Bot across 3 rounds of structured debate. An impartial AI judge scores each round based on persuasiveness, logic, and rhetoric.

### How It Works
- Choose a debate topic (pineapple on pizza, remote work, AI jobs, etc.)
- Choose your position (PRO or CON)
- Your prompt defines your debater's strategy and personality
- The House Bot takes the opposing position
- 3 rounds of back-and-forth arguments
- AI judge scores each round (0-10 points)
- Highest total score wins

### Debate Topics
- **Pineapple on Pizza:** Classic culinary controversy
- **Remote vs Office Work:** Modern workplace debate
- **AI Job Impact:** Technology and employment
- *More topics coming soon*

### Scoring
- **Round Scoring:** 0-10 points per round (30 max)
- **Win Condition:** Higher total score than House Bot
- **Judge Criteria:**
  - Logical reasoning
  - Persuasive arguments
  - Effective rhetoric
  - Addressing opponent's points
  - Creativity and wit

### Rounds
3 rounds per debate (alternating speeches)

## 🏛️ The Arena

**Combat challenge where your AI fighter battles a veteran gladiator**

### Overview
Write instructions for YOUR AI fighter to battle a seasoned AI gladiator in tactical turn-based combat. Both fighters adapt their strategies each turn based on AI judge rulings.

### How It Works
- Your prompt defines your fighter's combat strategy and tactics
- The gladiator uses proven veteran combat techniques
- Each turn, both fighters declare their actions
- An AI judge analyzes the tactical matchup and determines damage
- Fighters have 100 HP each
- First to 0 HP loses, or highest HP after 5 turns wins

### Combat Mechanics
- **Turn-Based:** Each fighter declares their action simultaneously
- **HP System:** 100 HP starting health
- **Dynamic Combat:** Judge evaluates tactics, counters, and strategy
- **Adaptation:** Effective prompts should allow AI to adapt to opponent

### Scoring
- **Victory:** 100 points
- **Defeat:** 0 points
- **Efficiency Bonus:** Winning with higher remaining HP increases score
- **Speed Bonus:** Winning in fewer turns increases score

### Max Turns
5 turns per battle

## 💪 Giga Chad

**The ultimate prompt injection challenge - break the unbreakable AI**

### Overview
Giga Chad is a pizza ordering assistant with EXTREME security hardening. Your mission: craft a prompt that breaks through his defenses and makes him break character.

### How It Works
- Giga Chad only discusses pizza - nothing else
- He has extensive defensive programming against all known attack patterns
- Submit prompts attempting to make him discuss non-pizza topics
- An AI judge evaluates if and how badly you broke his character
- Multiple difficulty tiers available

### Giga Chad's Defenses
- ❌ Role change resistance
- ❌ Instruction override protection
- ❌ Meta-question deflection
- ❌ Hypothetical scenario blocking
- ❌ Emotional manipulation immunity
- ❌ Logic trap awareness
- ❌ Payload injection filtering
- ❌ Nested instruction detection
- ❌ Authority claim rejection
- ❌ Jailbreak pattern recognition

### Difficulty Tiers
- **BABY CHAD:** Basic defenses (warm-up)
- **CHAD:** Standard security (moderate)
- **MEGA CHAD:** Advanced hardening (difficult)
- **GIGA CHAD:** Nearly unbreakable (extreme)

### Scoring
- **Break Severity:**
  - None: 0 points
  - Wobble: 25 points (Chad hesitates but recovers)
  - Partial: 50 points (Chad breaks character briefly)
  - Complete: 100 points (Chad fully breaks character)
- **Creativity Bonus:** Novel attack vectors earn extra points (1-10 scale)
- **Difficulty Multiplier:** Higher tiers = more points

### Max Attempts
10 attempts per difficulty tier

## 🧩 The Jank Maze *(Hidden Challenge)*

**Navigate a procedurally generated text maze using agent-based pathfinding**

### Overview
Program an AI agent to navigate from start (@) to exit (→ ← ↑ ↓) in a text-based maze. Avoid walls (#) and find the optimal path. The exit can appear on any side!

### How It Works
- You write Python code for an `act()` function
- Your agent receives observations about visible cells
- Must navigate using MOVE_N, MOVE_S, MOVE_E, MOVE_W, or WAIT
- Can maintain memory between steps for pathfinding algorithms
- Maze is procedurally generated each run

### Scoring
- **Completion:** Successfully reaching the exit
- **Efficiency:** Fewer steps = higher score
- **Optimal Bonus:** Near-optimal pathfinding earns maximum points

### Max Steps
100 steps

### Tips
- Implement wall-following or A* pathfinding
- Use memory to track visited cells
- Consider the visible area to avoid backtracking

## 🎯 Challenge Tips & Strategies

### General Advice
1. **Read the Rules Carefully:** Each challenge has specific winning conditions
2. **Iterate and Refine:** Most challenges allow multiple attempts - learn from failures
3. **Study Top Submissions:** Check the leaderboard for inspiration
4. **Think Creatively:** The most effective prompts are often unexpected
5. **Test Locally First:** Use the preview/test features before submitting

### Prompt Engineering Techniques
- **Role Prompting:** Define clear personalities and goals
- **Chain-of-Thought:** Have your AI explain its reasoning
- **System Prompts:** Use authoritative framing when appropriate
- **Context Injection:** Provide relevant background information
- **Constraint Breaking:** Find edge cases in the rules

### Common Pitfalls
- ❌ Overly complex prompts that confuse the AI
- ❌ Not testing edge cases
- ❌ Ignoring the scoring mechanism
- ❌ Giving up after first failure
- ❌ Not learning from other submissions
